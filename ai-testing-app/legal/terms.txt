Please read this in full before paying or beginning an attempt. By checking
the agreement box and paying, you accept every term below.

1. ADULTS ONLY
This service is available to adults (18 years or older) only. Do not use
this service if you are under 18. Vivacada offers other, human-proctored
testing options through partner institutions that may be available to
minors; this AI-proctored path is not one of them.

2. NO HUMAN INVOLVEMENT
Every part of this specific exam path — writing your interview questions,
grading your answers, and reviewing your recording afterward — is performed
by automated systems. No Vivacada staff member or any other human reviews
your exam content or your footage at any point, including if your attempt
is rejected. If you want a human-proctored exam instead, look for a
participating institution on Vivacada's site.

3. FEE AND PAYMENT
Each attempt costs $10.00 and is charged before your session begins. This
fee is the same whether it is your first attempt or a retry after a
rejection. Payment is not refundable for any reason, including a rejection
you believe was incorrect, a rejection caused by a system or technical
failure rather than anything you did, or simply changing your mind partway
through.

4. WHAT "REJECTED" MEANS AND WHAT HAPPENS NEXT
After you submit your exam, an automated system reviews your recording. It
checks, among other things, that a real face is visible, that a legible
government-issued photo ID was shown and its photo matches your face, that
you performed the requested gestures, and that your surroundings and
conduct during the exam appear legitimate. If any of these checks fails,
your attempt is rejected: none of your exam content or personal
information is sent to Vivacada, and you are told only that the attempt
was not approved, not the specific reason. If the system is unable to
complete this review after a limited number of attempts due to a technical
problem on our end, your attempt is also treated as unsuccessful under
this same policy. Either way, trying again requires paying the fee again.

5. THE AI CAN BE WRONG
Automated proctoring is not perfect. It may reject a legitimate attempt by
mistake — for example, because of lighting, camera angle, an unusual but
innocent movement, or a misread ID. By using this service, you accept this
risk and agree that a false rejection is not grounds for a refund. If this
is not an acceptable risk to you, please use a human-proctored option
instead.

6. RECORDING
Once you click "Start recording," your webcam and screen are recorded
continuously for the remainder of your attempt: while you show your face,
show your ID, perform the requested gestures, sweep your camera around
your room, and take the exam itself. If you retry a step (for example,
redoing the gestures), only your final take for that step is kept and
reviewed — earlier takes are discarded immediately.

7. YOUR ID
You will be asked to show a valid, legible government-issued photo ID.
The system reads the birthdate printed on it and compares your face in
that moment to the photo on the ID. If you do not show an ID, if it is not
legible, or if your face does not appear to match the ID photo, your
attempt is rejected. The birthdate read from your ID is used only to
determine, per credential, when it becomes eligible to be shared with
employers — it is never displayed on your credential or shared with
anyone else.

8. WHAT HAPPENS TO YOUR RECORDING
Your recording is used only for the automated review described above.
Every clip — webcam and screen, every step, including any discarded retry
takes — is permanently deleted immediately once that review is complete,
regardless of whether your attempt is approved or rejected. Vivacada does
not keep your video for any length of time and does not use it for
anything other than this one review.

9. WHAT GETS SENT TO VIVACADA
If your attempt is approved, only the following is sent to Vivacada to
create your credential: your email, the subject and level tested, your
exam transcript, your score and feedback, and the birthdate read from your
ID. Your recording itself is never sent anywhere and is deleted as
described above.

10. CHECKING YOUR STATUS
After you submit a passing exam, you'll receive a link to check whether it
has cleared automated review. This review happens without any further
action from you. If it is rejected, nothing is sent to Vivacada, and you
may begin a new, separately-paid attempt at any time.

11. ACCOUNT
A Vivacada account with the same email you use here is required to view
your credential once it's approved. If you don't have one yet, your result
is held until you create one with a matching email.
