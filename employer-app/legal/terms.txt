Anyone may create an account on this service; there is no manual approval
step. Please read this in full — by creating an account you agree to it.

1. WHO CAN USE THIS
This service is not restricted to hiring purposes. Legitimate uses include
recruiting and hiring, but also research, journalism, academic study, or
simple curiosity about what Vivacada credentials look like in aggregate.
What is not permitted is using this service for any malicious purpose,
including but not limited to: harassment, stalking, or targeting a specific
individual; discrimination on any legally protected basis; scraping or
bulk-exporting data for resale or redistribution; attempting to identify,
contact, or take action against a candidate outside of a good-faith hiring
or research process; or any use that violates applicable law.

2. WHAT YOU CAN SEE
Only credentials whose holder has both made the credential public and
separately opted in to data sharing are visible here at all, and only once
that person is old enough (per the birthdate recorded at test time) that
sharing their data is permitted. A holder's full exam transcript is visible
only if they have separately turned that on for that specific credential —
turning on data sharing does not by itself expose transcript content. You
never see more through this service than a member of the public could see
by visiting that person's public credential page directly; this service
makes that same public information searchable and browsable, it does not
expose anything additional.

3. SUBSCRIPTIONS AND BALANCE
Browsing requires an active subscription, billed on a recurring basis until
canceled. AI-assisted natural-language search is billed separately against
a balance you fund in advance; a search will never be allowed to proceed if
it might cost more than your remaining balance, so your balance can never
go negative. Subscription charges and balance funds are not refundable
except as required by law.

4. SPECIAL-CATALOG TEST SUBMISSIONS
You may propose a new subject for the special catalog for a fixed fee,
charged at the time you submit. This fee is not refunded if your submission
is not approved, regardless of the reason. In practice, a good-faith
submission describing a real subject is very likely to be approved,
reflecting genuine subject-matter interest, however niche; rejection is
reserved for content that is offensive or does not describe a coherent
subject to test.

5. YOUR API KEY
Your account is issued an API key automatically, usable for programmatic
access to every feature also available in this site's interface. You are
responsible for keeping it confidential. You may rotate it at any time from
your account settings; rotating immediately invalidates the previous key.

6. ENFORCEMENT
Vivacada may suspend or terminate any account for violating these terms,
including the prohibition on malicious use in Section 1, without refunding
any subscription payment, balance, or submission fee.
